# The Daily World — Tuesday 24 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Sum up 24 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124).

A newspaper front page, drawn entirely in code (PIL, no downloaded images):
the masthead, the date, and five stories from Tuesday 24 November 2026.

- **Lead: COP31 collapses in Antalya.** 90+ nations reject the final draft
  after almost 50 hours of overtime talks — it never mentions fossil fuels.
  Illustrated with a cracked globe drawn in code.
- **Ukraine:** a Ukrainian sea drone is said to have sunk the Russian
  corvette Merkury in Benghazi, Libya.
- **Nvidia:** Jensen Huang returns as CEO after Trump intervened; the US
  blacklists Megaspeed in the spy scandal.
- **Rohingya:** fourteen nations back the ICJ ruling on Myanmar's genocide
  of the Rohingya.
- **Nepal:** a magnitude 5.6 quake strikes near the border with China.

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`. The image is
1024x1024 PNG, well under the 1 MiB limit, and both runs write identical bytes.
